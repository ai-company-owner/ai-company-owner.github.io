Does it upload my EPUB files?

No. The supported workflow processes EPUB files locally, without sending manuscript content to an AI API.

Can it fix every EPUB error?

No. Only supported structural issues can be repaired. Other issues may require manual correction.

Does it replace my original EPUB?

No. The repair workflow writes a separate output and keeps the original file.
